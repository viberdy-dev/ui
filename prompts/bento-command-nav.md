The call to action is the only filled accent (text dark or white by the accent's luminance); the status dot is the only other colour.

Shared hairlines: the <nav> is a Tailwind `@container`, position relative, isolation isolate. On pointermove over the nav, coalesced to one write per animation frame, set --bcn-x/--bcn-y on every light layer from its own bounding rect (times offsetWidth / rect width, so it works inside scaled containers) and --bcn-o to 1 on the nav; on leave, cancel any pending frame and set it to 0.

Cells, in order: brand (14px, 600); each item (13px, 500, white 65%, hover raised and white; the current one raised, white, with a 4px white dot and aria-current="page"); an empty flex-1 tile, the row's negative space, holding the status right-aligned from @5xl (mono 11px with a 6px dot: ok #2bd576, busy #ffb020, down #ff5a5a); secondary; a Menu tile below @4xl; the CTA filled with the accent, with an arrow that slides 2px on hover. Behaviour: hover intent for mouse pointers only (open after 80ms, switch at once if another panel is open, close 180ms after leaving unless it was opened by click); click toggles; Escape closes and returns focus to the trigger; a pointerdown outside the nav or focus moving to another cell closes it.
