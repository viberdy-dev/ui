````text
Build a React + Tailwind card, `HaloTreatmentCard({ name, line, duration, price, href, glow, tag, tone, index })`, a treatment card with a light well.

A 26px-radius article (raised surface, inset hairline, feathered shadow). At the top, an 8px-inset well (aspect 5/4, 20px radius, panel colour) holding an oversized span: a radial gradient in the treatment's colour at 50% 72% (strong core, softer by 45%, gone by 72%) and an SVG grain tile in overlay blend masked to the same radial. At rest the light sits low at 80% opacity; on hover or focus-within it goes to full opacity, and pointermove sets its transform to lean toward the pointer (18% of the x offset, 12% of the y, lifted 18px, scale 1.14), transitioned over 420ms; leaving clears it. The card translates -4px on hover and focus. In the well: a serif italic two-digit index and a mono tag pill. Below: the name as an h3 whose link has an after pseudo-element covering the card, a description, and a footer row with duration and price in mono and a Book cue that nudges on hover. The focus ring goes on the card via .dg-card:has(a:focus-visible), with the link's own outline removed.

Diffused Glow grammar: two grounds, a warm bone day (#f6f1e6; panels #efe7d6, raised #fbf8f1; ink #221f1a at 92/58/40%; hairlines at 9% and 18%) and a near-black jade room (#0b120f; panels #101c17 and #16261f; ink #f3ede2 at 94/63/40%). Light is soft and grained: halos in gold #f5c48c, rose #f2a39a and, on the day ground, jade #bcd9c8 (or any #rrggbb validated against /^#[0-9a-fA-F]{6}$/), never more than two per view, each a wide gaussian skirt with a warmer core, and the grain lives inside the light (an SVG feTurbulence tile masked to the halo, or noise in the shader), never as a page overlay. In the jade room light is added (screen blend); on the day ground it pools as colour (the paper takes the tint). One bronze accent #c9a15e used as light and one jade fill #2f6f57 kept for the booking action; one filled action per view. A serif (Fraunces by default, through var(--font-serif, "Fraunces", Georgia, serif)) at 340-400 for display with 0.98-1.04 leading and *italic* accents, over Geist for UI and Geist Mono uppercase captions. Radii 24-28px on panels, pills for controls; feathered shadows only. Motion is slow: halos breathe on about 10s, drift on 36s or longer and settle toward the pointer over about 900ms (1 - e^(-3dt)); resolves run 700-1100ms out of a blur on cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1); hovers under 450ms. Keyframes and a focus ring (a .dg-scope rule with border-radius: revert-layer) live in a small static <style> the component renders itself; nothing is interpolated into it. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. No dependencies beyond React.
````
