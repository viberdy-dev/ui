# Wobble Drop Card: AI prompt

Paste this into Cursor, Claude Code, v0, Lovable or Bolt to generate **Wobble Drop Card** from scratch, or edit it first to restyle it. The finished code is [components/wobble-drop-card.tsx](../components/wobble-drop-card.tsx); the live demo is on [viberdy.dev](https://viberdy.dev/library/wobble-drop-card?ref=github).

````text
Build a React + Tailwind card, `WobbleDropCard({ drop, onAdd, className })` where drop = { title, artist, price, formats, sticker, stickerTone, cover: { bg, fg, shape: 'dot' | 'wave' | 'stack' }, href, cta }.

Tactile Maximalism grammar: loud but composed. A dough ground #f7f0e4 over most of the page (surfaces #fffaf1, a pressed well #efe5d3), warm ink #17130f at 92/64/42%, hairlines at 10% and 18%; ONE loud accent, bubblegum #ff2d78 (deep #d8155d), as a filled thing once per screen, and one quiet tint, lavender #e8e1f6, only on small chips, never a second loud colour. Grain at 4-6%, monochrome, static, fixed to the material. Depth is soft and tinted: a contact shadow 0 2px 6px rgba(23,19,15,0.1) plus a wide one 0 18px 40px -8px tinted by what casts it (a gum chip casts pink); pressed, both collapse to 0 1px 3px rgba(23,19,15,0.14). Never a hard offset shadow (that is neo-brutalism). Type: Unbounded at 800 through var(--font-unbounded, "Unbounded", ...) for one oversized headline per screen (up to ~15cqw, leading 0.92, tracking -0.045em), Geist for reading at 16-17px/1.5, Geist Mono uppercase at 11-12px for labels. Full pills for every control and chip, 28px cards, 20px nested media. Motion: everything soft keeps its area as it squashes (scaleX = 1 / scaleY about its foot), deforms in proportion to the input (where, how long, how fast), overshoots once and settles inside 450ms (a spring near k 420, c 18), and always returns to one legible rest shape. Reduced motion keeps the states and drops the springs.

A 28px card (surface #fffaf1, 1px 10% hairline, the soft two-layer shadow) holding a square cover (20px radius, drawn in code: a big circle, four layered SVG waves, or a pile of three cushions askew, over grain) with a round 76px sticker on its top right corner rotated -12 degrees (gum, ink or lilac, Unbounded 700 11.5px), then the title (a link, Unbounded 700 18px), the artist, the price, lilac mono format chips and a small ink squash button. Physics in one rAF loop that sleeps when settled: the lean is a spring (k 170, c 18) toward rotateX(-12 * py) rotateY(12 * px) from the pointer's position over the card (perspective 900px); the sticker's angle is a spring (k 260, c 9) driven by the card's lean velocity, so it swings against the motion and jiggles out. Press (not on the link or button) squashes the inner card to 0.93 with scale(1/y, y) about its base on the kit's spring and kicks the sticker; leave or release restores it.
````
