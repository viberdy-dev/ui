````text
Create a React + Tailwind v4 client component, BentoFooterGrid: a site footer set as dark bento tiles whose tiles are live: a 90-day uptime strip you can read day by day, a back-to-top ring that fills with scroll progress, a newsletter tile that expands across its row with a FLIP reflow of its neighbours, and a cropped wordmark that rises into its crop with scroll.

Types (export them): FooterLink { label, href }; FooterColumn { title, links }; UptimeDay { uptime (0-100), date? ("yyyy-mm-dd"), note? }; FooterStatus { label, value? (defaults to the mean of days, floored to two decimals), caption?, href? (makes the label a link with ↗), tone: "ok" | "busy" | "down", days? (up to 90, oldest first, the last is today) }. Props: brand { name, pitch?, href?, mark? (default a 28px white rounded square with the first letter) }, socials?, columns (three or four), status?, newsletter? { title, placeholder?, cta?, onSubscribe(email) (resolve when stored, throw to show the error) }, legal? { owner, year? (default this year), links? }, backToTop (default true), wordmark (default false), accent ("#rrggbb", regex-validated, default #ff5a1f), scroll ("page" default: follow the window; "self": follow the nearest ancestor with overflow-y auto or scroll, for fixed-height frames), className. Sanitise hrefs: strip tabs and newlines, reject backslashes, allow http(s), mailto, tel and relative paths starting / # ? but not //.

Also export FOOTER_DEMO, the one content object for a fictional build tool (brand, socials, three columns, status with days, newsletter title, legal), with a doc comment saying every name, link and figure is a placeholder and the uptime history is invented from a seed and must never ship as a status. Generate the days with a deterministic mulberry32 generator (identical on server and client): mostly 100, about 7% minor incidents (99.2-99.95, with a short note), about 1.5% major (97.6-98.9), today always 100.

Kit tokens: canvas #08090a painted by the footer itself; tiles #0f1012 with an inset 1px hairline at white 0.08, radius 20; raised #16171a on hover; gap 12; grid-auto-rows minmax(104px, auto), dense; mono labels 11px uppercase +0.12em at 50% white; figures bold grotesque with tabular numbers; the subscribe button is the only accent (text dark or white by luminance); the status tone (ok #2bd576, busy #ffb020, down #ff5a5a) is semantic and confined to the dot, today's bar and the headline figure. Focus rings are important utilities (focus-visible:![outline-style:solid], ![outline-width:2px], a per-element ![outline-offset] and ![border-radius]) with the colour inline, so a host's unlayered :focus-visible rule cannot replace them.

Layout: a <footer> that is a Tailwind @container, content max-width 1180px, a <ul role="list"> grid of 2 columns, 6 at @4xl. The brand is the one 2x2 (mark and name as a link, the pitch, socials as hairline chips in a list labelled Social). Each column is a 1x2 tile with a <nav aria-label>. With three columns the back-to-top tile is a tall 1x2 beside them; with four it is a 1x1 in the next row. Then, in DOM order: the newsletter (3 wide), the status (3 wide, or 2 with four columns), back to top if it is in this row, the legal line (6 wide), the wordmark (full width). Use literal class maps for the container spans (@4xl:col-span-1 ... @4xl:col-span-6) so the classes exist in source.

Status tile: a top row with the dot and label (left) and the figure at 26px bold in the tone (right); a strip of one bar per day (flex, 2px gaps, 28px tall, bars end-aligned): full days full height at white 0.16, minor days 58% at 0.5, major days 32% at 0.5, today's bar in the tone with a class that runs a 10-second pulse (steady, one dip to 0.35 opacity near 82%), paused unless the footer carries data-bfg-live and off under reduced motion; a bottom row with the readout (the caption by default) and "Today" at the right. The strip is role="slider" (tabIndex 0, valuemin 1, valuemax n, valuenow and valuetext naming the day): pointer position maps to a day (a hovered bar goes full height and bright), arrows, Page Up/Down (7 days), Home and End move it, keyboard focus starts at today, the readout shows "12 days ago · 99.68% · Build queue delayed" (or the date as "12 Aug"), and a mouse leaving clears it. touch-action pan-y.

Back to top: a real button on a layer 1px inside the tile (hover #16171a), a mono label, a 52px SVG ring (track white 0.1, progress white 0.85, 1.5px, pathLength 1, dasharray 1 1, rotated -90deg) around a ↑ that rises 4px on hover, and a mono percentage. Progress = scrollTop / (scrollHeight - clientHeight) of the scroller; write the ring's stroke-dashoffset and the percentage directly on scroll (passive listener, coalesced to one animation frame, only while the footer is on screen per an IntersectionObserver whose root is the scroller). The click scrolls the scroller to the top with behavior "smooth", or "instant" under reduced motion (never "auto": hosts often set scroll-behavior smooth on html).

Wordmark: a full-width, aria-hidden tile (itself a container) with the brand name in bold at min(150 / max(3, name length) cqw capped at 40cqw, 420px), colour #16171a, leading 0.8, tracking -0.06em. It is scroll-linked: in the same scroll handler, q = (viewport bottom - tile top) / tile height clamped 0-1, and translateY goes from 64% (q 0) to 18% (q 1), so it rises into its crop as the footer arrives and sinks when you scroll back; fixed at 18% under reduced motion.

Newsletter expand (FLIP): the newsletter tile and the legal tile paint a separate "skin" (absolute, left 0, top 0, 100% by 100%, radius 20, the tile surface and hairline, the pointer lights) so their content stays in flow. Focus anywhere in the newsletter sets wide; blur to outside it collapses only if the field is empty; a successful subscribe collapses it. Before changing state, record every [data-flip] element's box in grid coordinates (sum offsetLeft/Top up to the grid plus any in-flight transform; a skin tile is measured by its skin). In a layout effect, cancel running animations, then: a skin tile animates its skin's left, top, width and height from the old box (relative to its new position) to its new size; any other flip element (the status, back to top, the wordmark, and the inner title, form, copyright and links, which each carry data-flip) animates transform from the old offset to none with z-index 2. Opening uses 380ms on cubic-bezier(0.34,1.25,0.64,1), closing 300ms on cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1); nothing animates under reduced motion.

Newsletter form: noValidate, a visually hidden label, an email input (type email, inputMode email, autoComplete email) and the submit inside a 44px field (#131417, radius 14, a white 0.4 inset ring on focus-within, red when invalid); states idle, busy ("Joining…", disabled), done (the form is replaced by a check and a role="status" message), invalid and error, with messages in a role="status" line referenced by aria-describedby and aria-invalid on the input.

Light: on pointermove over the grid, coalesced to one write per animation frame, write each item's --bfg-x/--bfg-y (layout pixels via the rect-to-offsetWidth ratio, minus its offsets) and --bfg-o 1 on the grid (0 on leave); every tile has a ring of radial-gradient(180px circle, white 0.7 → transparent 70%) through a 1px padding mask (mask-composite exclude) and a glow of radial 260px white 0.07, both at opacity var(--bfg-o) with 200ms transitions.

Output one self-contained "use client" TSX file with no dependencies beyond React. Build strings with + rather than template literals.
````
