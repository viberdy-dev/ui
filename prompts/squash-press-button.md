Build a React + Tailwind button, `SquashPressButton({ children, href, onClick, type, variant, size, disabled, ariaLabel, className })`.

Tactile Maximalism grammar: loud but composed. A dough ground #f7f0e4 over most of the page (surfaces #fffaf1, a pressed well #efe5d3), warm ink #17130f at 92/64/42%, hairlines at 10% and 18%; ONE loud accent, bubblegum #ff2d78 (deep #d8155d), as a filled thing once per screen, and one quiet tint, lavender #e8e1f6, only on small chips, never a second loud colour. Grain at 4-6%, monochrome, static, fixed to the material. Depth is soft and tinted: a contact shadow 0 2px 6px rgba(23,19,15,0.1) plus a wide one 0 18px 40px -8px tinted by what casts it (a gum chip casts pink); pressed, both collapse to 0 1px 3px rgba(23,19,15,0.14). Never a hard offset shadow (that is neo-brutalism). Type: Unbounded at 800 through var(--font-unbounded, "Unbounded", ...) for one oversized headline per screen (up to ~15cqw, leading 0.92, tracking -0.045em), Geist for reading at 16-17px/1.5, Geist Mono uppercase at 11-12px for labels. Full pills for every control and chip, 28px cards, 20px nested media. Motion: everything soft keeps its area as it squashes (scaleX = 1 / scaleY about its foot), deforms in proportion to the input (where, how long, how fast), overshoots once and settles inside 450ms (a spring near k 420, c 18), and always returns to one legible rest shape. Reduced motion keeps the states and drops the springs.

The hit area (a button, or a link when href is given) holds an inner pill span that squashes, so the hit area never moves. A hook `useSquish(ref)` runs one spring on the pill's height factor y (k 420, c 18, three substeps a frame) and one on a skew (k 380, c 20), writing transform: scale(1/y, y) skewX(skew) straight to the element, and stops when settled. Pointer down sets the transform origin to the pressed x (15-85%) at the bottom and presses to 0.9, deepening to 0.8 over a 600ms hold; up or cancel releases to 1 (the spring overshoots once). Leaving with a quick sideways swipe flicks the skew by the pointer's speed. Enter (and Space on a button) presses; key up and blur release. Shadows: rest = a 0 2px 6px contact at 10% plus a 0 18px 40px -8px tinted by the pill's colour at 20%; hover widens it (x1.3); pressed = 0 1px 3px at 14%; transition 180ms. Inks: gum (#ff2d78, paper text, pink shadow), ink (#17130f, paper text), soft (#fffaf1 with a 10% hairline, ink text). Label in Unbounded 600, -0.01em; sizes lg 56px, md 44px, sm 36px tall.
