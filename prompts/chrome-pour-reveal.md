````text
Build a React + Tailwind wrapper, `ChromePourReveal({ children, delay, radius, drips, finish })`, that reveals its content by pouring liquid chrome over it.

Phases: wait, pour, cool, done. Measure the wrapper (ResizeObserver). An IntersectionObserver (root from a scroll context if there is one) starts the pour, after `delay`, once a third is visible. While waiting or pouring, the children are at opacity 0; they are always rendered.

The overlay (aria-hidden, pointer-events none, clipped with the given radius) holds an SVG the size of the wrapper: a vertical chrome linearGradient in user space (the four-band ramp times the finish) and a goo filter (feGaussianBlur of about 2% of the width, then an feColorMatrix alpha of 22a - 10, used on its own so the bridges between drips keep their colour). Inside the filtered group: a sheet rect slightly larger than the box and `drips` rounded bars at seeded columns (seeded from the instance id), each with its own width, length and lead. Pour with the Web Animations API over 860ms (ease in-out): the sheet from translateY(-115%) to 0, and each drip keyed to the sheet's front, hanging ahead of it by a lead that grows from 25% to 90% of its length. Then cool over 700ms: animate the overlay's mask-position so a transparent-to-opaque gradient (250% tall) wipes the metal away from the top, with a glowing 2px seam, outside the mask, riding the edge. Then remove the overlay. Reduced motion: done at once.

Liquid Chrome grammar: a graphite room (ground #0c0d10, panels #141519 and #1b1c21, hairlines white 7% and 16% drawn as inset box-shadows or SVG strokes, never border colours), ink #f4f5f7 at 94/62/40%. Chrome is a reflected room, never a flat grey gradient: four bands (#08090b, #4b4f58, #c9ced6, #ffffff) arranged as a bright sky over a hard dark horizon over a lit floor, multiplied by a finish (chrome #ffffff, gold #f2cf92, rose #f1bcb4, cobalt #b9cbff, or any #rrggbb validated against /^#[0-9a-fA-F]{6}$/) and handed to CSS as --lc-0 to --lc-3 with the horizon position in --lc-h. Colour appears only as a thin film on Newton's series (silver #d8dce2, champagne #eadfc4, magenta #d68fc7, cyan #8fd6d1, green #a8d68f, gold #d6b98f, violet #b48fd6, unevenly spaced), never a hue-rotate rainbow. Chrome goes only on heavy, curved forms (display type, pills, discs), never on hairlines. Display type is Archivo at 800 with font-stretch 125% and -0.02em tracking through var(--font-display, "Archivo", ...); UI in Geist; captions in Geist Mono, uppercase. Pills and 26px radii. The room eases toward the pointer in about 220ms, ambient drift runs 40s or slower, presses squash about 4% in 130ms. Keyframes and a focus ring (a .lc-scope rule with border-radius: revert-layer) live in a small static <style> the component renders itself; nothing is interpolated into it. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. No dependencies beyond React.
````
