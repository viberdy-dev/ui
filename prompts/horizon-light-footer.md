Build a React + Tailwind footer, `HorizonLightFooter({ brand, columns, status, legal, accent, wordmark, pulse })`, on #050505.

The horizon is a signal: measure the band's width and draw an SVG latency trace along its bottom (points every 4px; a near-flat line with slight noise plus a seeded handful of triangular request spikes up to about 25px). Stroke it in white 14%, then again with a userSpaceOnUse linear gradient whose five stops (transparent, accent, white, accent, transparent) sit around the pointer position, updating their offsets every frame. Three more copies of the trace carry the request pulse (dashes of 70, 40 and 8px: blurred accent glow, accent core, white head), positioned by strokeDashoffset from the pulse's progress so their heads stay aligned.

Motion in one rAF loop, only while visible: --hx eases toward the pointer's x (after 2.5s without movement it swings as 0.5 + 0.3 * sin(2 * pi * t / 40)); every `pulse` seconds a pulse travels the trace in 2.6s, driving the dash offsets, --px and --pa (sin-shaped, peaking at 0.75) so the letters brighten above it.
