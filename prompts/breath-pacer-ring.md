Build a React + Tailwind component, `BreathPacer({ inhale, hold, exhale, cycles, tone, glow, size, labels })`, a breathing guide in soft light.

A square stage (`size` px): a hairline circle, a halo span at inset 6% (a closest-side radial in the light colour plus a masked grain tile) scaled 0.55 to 1.0, an SVG ring (r 46 in a 100 viewBox, rotated -90deg, 0.6 stroke) whose dash offset traces the current phase, and centred text (the phase word in italic serif, the seconds in mono; at rest a mono line 'In for 4 · out for 6'). Below, a quiet SoftGlowButton (Begin / Pause / Once more) and a mono 'n / total'. While running, one rAF loop reads performance.now() (one clock), splits the cycle into in, hold and out, sets the halo's scale with a sine ease (0.55 + 0.45 ease on the inhale, 1 during the hold, back down on the exhale) and the ring's dash offset, and sets state only when the phase, the whole second or the cycle changes. Keep an elapsed offset in a ref so Pause resumes; after `cycles` breaths stop and show the closing line. Announce each phase once in a polite sr-only status. Reduced motion: no scaling.

Diffused Glow grammar: two grounds, a warm bone day (#f6f1e6; panels #efe7d6, raised #fbf8f1; ink #221f1a at 92/58/40%; hairlines at 9% and 18%) and a near-black jade room (#0b120f; panels #101c17 and #16261f; ink #f3ede2 at 94/63/40%). A serif (Fraunces by default, through var(--font-serif, "Fraunces", Georgia, serif)) at 340-400 for display with 0.98-1.04 leading and *italic* accents, over Geist for UI and Geist Mono uppercase captions.
